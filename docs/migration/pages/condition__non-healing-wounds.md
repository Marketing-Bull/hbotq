# Non-Healing Wounds

Non-healing wounds, affecting around 6.5 million Americans annually, are wounds that fail to heal within about three months, often affecting individuals with conditions like diabetes and obesity. Chronic wounds include leg and foot ulcers, as well as pressure ulcers (bedsores).

Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy (HBOT) helps accelerate the healing process by improving blood flow to the affected area, making it particularly effective in treating diabetes-related foot ulcers.

**[Video]** https://hbotq.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Non-Healing-Wounds-and-Hyperbaric-Oxygen-Therapy.mp4

## HBOT For Non-Healing Wounds

Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy (HBOT) provides key benefits for patients with non-healing wounds, including:

  * Reducing the risk of amputation in diabetic patients
  * Killing anaerobic bacteria in the wound area
  * Decreasing the size of certain chronic wounds

These effects help improve wound healing and promote recovery.
