# Post Covid


Some people with long COVID experience lingering symptoms such as fatigue, brain fog, anxiety, and muscle pain, which can significantly impact daily life. Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy (HBOT) has emerged as a potential treatment by increasing oxygen levels in the bloodstream, reducing inflammation, and promoting healing. HBOT works by stimulating anti-inflammatory cytokines and growth factors, as well as boosting the expression of anti-inflammatory genes. Given the role of inflammation in long COVID, HBOT may offer relief for those experiencing persistent symptoms.

## HBOT For Long Covid

Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy (HBOT) offers several benefits for patients with post-COVID conditions:

  * Decreases inflammation
  * Enhances immune system function
  * Promotes stem cell mobilization
  * Accelerates repair, recovery, and potential tissue regeneration

These effects help alleviate lingering symptoms and improve overall recovery from long COVID.
