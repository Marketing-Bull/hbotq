# Diabetic Lower Extremity Wounds

Diabetes is a metabolic disorder that impairs the body’s ability to produce enough insulin, leading to elevated blood glucose levels, which hinder wound healing and increase the risk of infection. Diabetic ulcers, particularly on the legs and feet, can become chronic if not healed within three months. Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy (HBOT) accelerates the healing of these lower extremity wounds, lowers amputation rates, and significantly improves the quality of life for diabetic patients.

**[Video]** https://hbotq.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Diabetic-Lower-Extremity-Wounds-and-Hyperbaric-Oxygen-Therapy.mp4

## HBOT For Diabetic Wounds

Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy (HBOT) benefits patients with diabetic lower extremity wounds by:

  * Fighting infections
  * Reducing pain
  * Promoting the formation of new blood vessels
  * Accelerating repair and recovery with increased oxygen supply
  * Stimulating stem cell mobilization
  * Reducing inflammation

These effects help speed up healing and improve outcomes for chronic diabetic wounds.
