# Chronic Pain

Chronic pain is defined as pain lasting over six months, ranging from mild to severe, and can be constant or intermittent. It affects both physical and emotional health due to its prolonged impact on the nervous system.

Hyperbaric oxygen therapy (HBOT) has shown effectiveness in reducing chronic pain, particularly in conditions like fibromyalgia, complex regional pain syndrome, myofascial pain syndrome, migraines, and cluster headaches. By increasing oxygen levels in the body, HBOT helps alleviate pain and promote healing.

**[Video]** https://hbotq.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Chronic-Pain-and-Hyperbaric-Oxygen-Therapy.mp4

## HBOT For Chronic Pain

Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy (HBOT) provides several benefits for chronic pain patients:

  * Reduces pain and tenderness
  * Decreases inflammation
  * Fights infection
  * Promotes the formation of new blood vessels

These effects help alleviate discomfort and support the body’s natural healing processes.
