# Radiation Tissue Damage


Radiation treatment, commonly used for cancer, can cause necrosis, damaging both healthy and cancerous cells. This damage may lead to non-healing wounds due to impaired blood supply, particularly in delayed radiation injuries that appear six months or more after treatment. Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy (HBOT) helps repair radiation-damaged tissue by promoting blood vessel regeneration, stimulating stem cell growth, and accelerating wound healing. The benefits of HBOT in treating radiation-induced tissue damage have been demonstrated in several controlled studies.

## HBOT For Radiation Tissue Damage

Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy (HBOT) offers several benefits for patients with radiation tissue damage:

  * Helps fight infection
  * Reduces pain
  * Accelerates repair and recovery by increasing oxygen supply
  * Promotes stem cell mobilization
  * Reduces inflammation

These combined effects aid in healing damaged tissue and improving overall recovery outcomes.
